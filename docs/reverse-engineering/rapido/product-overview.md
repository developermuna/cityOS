# Rapido Product Overview & Market Architecture

## 1. Product Positioning
Rapido is an Indian mobility platform initially popularized as a two-wheeler bike taxi aggregator that expanded into three-wheeler auto rickshaws, cabs (economy hatchback, sedan, SUV), and parcel delivery.

## 2. Core Value Propositions
- **Speed & Traffic Agility**: Bike taxi as an affordable, solo-commuter solution in high-density urban areas.
- **Transparent Meterless/Negotiation-Free Auto**: Fixed upfront fares for three-wheelers eliminating street haggling.
- **Multimodal Fleet**: Bike, Auto, Cab Economy (Mini), Cab Comfort (Sedan), and Parcel in a unified app.
- **Sachet Affordability**: Lower ticket size compared to traditional cab aggregators.
- **Low Driver Churn Model / Zero Commission Variations**: SaaS or subscription-based models for drivers (Captains) vs variable commission.

## 3. Vehicle Categories & Tiers
| Tier | Vehicle Type | Capacity | Primary Use Case | Observable Pricing Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bike** | Two-wheeler motorcycle / scooter | 1 passenger + helmet | Fast solo commute, short-to-medium trips, traffic bypass | Lowest base fare, per km rate ₹8-₹12 |
| **Auto** | 3-Wheeler LPG/CNG/Electric Rickshaw | Up to 3 passengers | Everyday errands, weather-protected city commute | Mid-tier base fare, per km rate ₹12-₹16 |
| **Cab Economy** | Hatchback (WagonR, Indica, Tiago) | Up to 4 passengers | Budget group travel, AC comfort | Higher base fare, per km rate ₹15-₹20 |
| **Cab Comfort** | Sedan (Dzire, Aura, Etios) | Up to 4 passengers | Executive travel, luggage, premium AC | Premium base fare, per km rate ₹18-₹25 |
| **Parcel** | Bike / 2-Wheeler | Up to 5 kg packages | Intra-city item and document delivery | Distance + package weight tiers |

## 4. Key Observable Product Mechanisms
- **High-Frequency Captain Ping**: Realtime driver proximity updates on home map prior to booking.
- **Instant OTP Verification**: A 4-digit code provided to the passenger, entered by the driver to start the trip.
- **Masked VoIP / Virtual Number Routing**: Rider and driver communicate without revealing private mobile numbers.
- **Live Trip Telemetry**: Dynamic GPS track rendering with speed estimation and ETA recomputation.
- **Safety Shield**: High-contrast SOS button, direct police integration, emergency contact SMS broadcast with live tracking link.
